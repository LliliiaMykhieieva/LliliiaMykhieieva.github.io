02 / Selected Work

Four projects. Four different business problems.

01

Cancer Support Netherlands

Patient Navigator · Employer Partner Project

Business problem: international cancer patients in the Netherlands face healthcare information fragmented across Dutch-language sources, making it difficult to understand where to go, what to ask and what to do next.

Translated 26 patient needs into analytical requirements and built a traceable English knowledge base containing 1,378 records from trusted healthcare sources. I then validated 260 semantic-search results to assess information coverage and identify gaps around waiting times, English-speaking providers and practical patient support.

Business value: converted fragmented source information into evidence-based priorities and requirements for CSN's future Patient Navigator.

02

NHS England

Appointment & Capacity Analysis

Business problem: how can appointment data help identify pressure points in NHS demand, access and workforce capacity?

Analysed appointment data from 2020–2022 to investigate demand, workforce utilisation, appointment patterns and regional variation across NHS networks.

The analysis identified peak-demand periods, high-volume regions and limited spare capacity, including more than 1.5 million missed appointments in October 2021.

Business value: translated operational patterns into recommendations around workforce planning, appointment management and resource allocation.

03

Turtle Games

Customer & Loyalty Analytics

Business problem: how can customer behaviour be used to improve loyalty, engagement and marketing decisions?

Analysed 2,000 customer records using linear regression, decision trees, K-means clustering, NLP and statistical analysis.

The analysis showed that spending behaviour was more useful for customer targeting than demographics and identified 330 high-income, low-spending customers — 16.5% of the customer base — as a specific growth opportunity.

Business value: converted model outputs into practical CRM, retention and personalised-reward strategies rather than treating predictive modelling as an end in itself.

04

2Market

Marketing & Customer Analytics

Business problem: how can customer, product and campaign data reveal which markets, customer groups and advertising channels should receive greater marketing attention?

Cleaned and explored customer and marketing data in Excel, structured and joined datasets using PostgreSQL and SQL, and developed an interactive Tableau dashboard for decision-making.

Analysed customer spending by category and age group, country performance, customer response rates and advertising-channel conversions across markets.

Business value: brought fragmented customer and campaign metrics into a single BI view, making it easier to compare markets, understand customers and evaluate marketing effectiveness.
